Köhler illumination is a widely used technique in microscopy for illuminating the specimen evenly (see the video below). It is usually set up in transmission. However, spectro-microscopists often use reflected-light imaging, which relies on epi-illumination: the objective both illuminates the sample and collects the light coming back from it.
