**Ph.D. Candidate \| Physical & Materials Chemistry.** Investigating nanoscale light–matter interactions via single-particle optical spectroscopy to establish fundamental design principles for next-generation optoelectronic devices.

  * Advisor: Prof. Pramod Pillai

Research experience
======
* **2024–present: University of Notre Dame**, Indiana, USA
  * Fourier-space optical imaging of exciton–plasmon coupling in hybrid nanostructures
  * Built a custom Fourier-space imaging setup to map energy–momentum dispersion; determined polariton propagation lengths, group velocities, and dephasing times
  * Electromagnetic simulations (COMSOL Multiphysics) of energy-transfer pathways and radiation damping

* **2023–2024: IISER Pune**, Maharashtra, India
  * Thermal evolution of the structure and luminescence of the hybrid-cation-stabilized [(4AMTP)PbBr<sub>2</sub>]<sub>2</sub>PbBr<sub>4</sub> layered perovskite
  * Low-temperature (8–300 K) SCXRD/PXRD and variable-temperature steady-state and time-resolved spectroscopy of exciton dynamics

* **2022–2023: IISER Pune**, Maharashtra, India
  * Te<sup>4+</sup>/Er<sup>3+</sup> co-doped Cs<sub>2</sub>NaInCl<sub>6</sub> double perovskites for short-wave infrared (SWIR) emission
  * Hydrothermal synthesis, PXRD/ICP-MS characterization, temperature-dependent photoluminescence, and pc-LED prototype fabrication
